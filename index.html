Why QuickAid?

Blood requests are usually scattered across WhatsApp forwards, hospital notice boards, and social media — QuickAid puts it all in one searchable place.

  • Search donors instantly by blood group and city
  • Register in under a minute — no login required
  • Emergency helpline numbers always visible
  • Works fully offline once loaded — your data stays on your device
